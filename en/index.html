Omar Manky

I am a sociologist and I study work. I am interested in understanding what people and organizations do when the rules, the places or the technologies they work with change.

I have followed that question among mining unions in Peru, in the 2020 farmworkers' strike, in universities and in the cars of app drivers in Lima. I now follow it into offices where artificial intelligence is beginning to change the way people work. I teach at the Universidad del Pacífico.
